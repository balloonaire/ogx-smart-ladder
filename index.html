Session settings

Court order is highest to lowest. Winners ascend one court; losers descend one court. Top-court winners defend for at most 2 consecutive rounds, then descend directly to the lowest-ranked court and mix with new partners.

Use the arrows to move any physical court number to TOP or lower ranks. The order is saved automatically. Reordering is disabled while matches are live.

Rules apply to new and ongoing matches. Score completion requires manual confirmation. A match is finished when the target and margin are reached, or the optional cap is reached.

Players & waiting queue

Attendance: Pending / SHOW / NO SHOW. Only SHOW players enter match selection. Rest removes a waiting player from match selection until resumed. Sub lower prioritizes an eligible waiting player for the next match on the lowest court; live matches are never interrupted.
